# Vivox Voice Chat Integration

Complete guide to add voice chat to your multiplayer game.

---

## 🎤 What is Vivox?

Vivox is Unity's integrated voice chat solution for multiplayer games. It provides:
- ✅ Low-latency voice communication
- ✅ Automatic player spatial audio (3D positional voice)
- ✅ Push-to-talk or always-on modes
- ✅ Mute/unmute controls
- ✅ Volume controls per player
- ✅ Works cross-platform (PC, Mobile, Console)

---

## 📦 Installation

### 1. Enable in Unity Dashboard

1. Go to [Unity Dashboard](https://dashboard.unity.com/)
2. Select your project
3. Navigate to **Multiplayer > Vivox**
4. Click **"Enable Vivox"**
5. Accept the terms of service
6. Note your **Vivox Credentials** (auto-configured)
